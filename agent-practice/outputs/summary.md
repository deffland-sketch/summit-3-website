# Summary of documents in inputs/

_Three documents were read, all about the Compass mentoring pilot for ninth graders at Hillcrest: steering committee notes, a pilot update memo, and an email thread with a community partner._

## Main themes
- The mentor block structure is holding. The Tuesday and Thursday block has survived the fall, 84 of 90 students have met their mentor at least twice, and block attendance is 96 percent (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md).
- What happens inside the block is inconsistent. Mentors split among goal-setting, homework help, and "open time," and students of open-time mentors mostly cannot name a goal (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md).
- There is no agreed measure of whether students are "known well." The check-in log shows what mentors did, not what students experienced (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md).
- Whether to accept outside volunteer mentors in the spring is unresolved, with concern that a second kind of mentor could confuse students and families (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md, 03-partner-email-thread.md).
- The mentor role is not clearly defined for families. Some families have asked whether the mentor replaces the counselor (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 03-partner-email-thread.md).

## Open questions
- Should Hillcrest accept volunteer mentors from community partners in the spring? Leo is bringing it to the October 23 meeting, and Bridgeway will hold its twelve volunteers only until October 31 (03-partner-email-thread.md).
- If volunteers are accepted, will they meet inside the mentor block or at a separate time, who will supervise them, and whose background check process applies? (03-partner-email-thread.md)
- What should "known well" be measured by, a student survey or the check-in log? (01-steering-meeting-notes.md)
- Should a student survey be pursued this semester or wait until spring? Priya asked leadership for a decision by the end of October (02-pilot-update-memo.md).
- How should the mentor role be described to families so it is distinct from the counselor role? Ines is drafting this (01-steering-meeting-notes.md).

## Decisions named
- Keep the Tuesday and Thursday mentor block through the end of the semester with no timing changes, decided by the steering committee, with Marcus noted (01-steering-meeting-notes.md).
- Every mentor will use the same goal-setting protocol starting in October, decided by Priya. The memo describes it as a five-question protocol (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md).
- Dana will add a field to the check-in log recording whether the protocol was used. The memo reports this "protocol used" field has been added (01-steering-meeting-notes.md, 02-pilot-update-memo.md).
