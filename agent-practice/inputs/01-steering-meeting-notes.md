# Steering committee notes: Compass mentoring pilot

_Sample document for agent practice. The organization, people, and numbers are fictional._

**Date:** September 18
**Attendees:** Priya (project lead), Marcus (school principal), Dana (data and systems), Leo (community partnerships), Ines (family liaison)

## Where the pilot stands

Priya opened with the headline: 84 of the 90 ninth graders at Hillcrest have met with their mentor at least twice since the start of the year. The six who have not are all students who enrolled after the first week. Marcus said the schedule change that created the Tuesday and Thursday mentor block is holding, and teachers have stopped asking whether it will be pulled for testing.

## Discussion

Mentor time is being used unevenly. Dana shared the check-in log: about a third of mentors use the block for structured goal-setting conversations, a third use it for homework help, and a third describe it as "open time." Marcus and Priya both said that the open-time group is where they worry students are not being known well.

Leo raised the partner question again. Two community organizations have asked to place volunteer mentors at Hillcrest in the spring. Nobody on the committee has decided whether outside mentors fit this year's pilot or whether they would confuse the model.

Ines reported that families want a clearer picture of what the mentor does. Three families have asked whether the mentor replaces the counselor. She said the September family letter did not answer that.

## Decisions

- The committee decided to keep the Tuesday and Thursday block through the end of the semester with no changes to timing. (Marcus)
- Priya decided that every mentor will use the same short goal-setting protocol starting in October, and Dana will add a field to the check-in log to record whether it was used.

## Open items

- Whether to accept volunteer mentors from community partners in the spring. Leo to bring more detail in October.
- How to describe the mentor role to families in a way that separates it from the counselor role. Ines to draft.
- What "known well" should actually be measured by. Dana asked whether a student survey or the check-in log is the better source and no one answered.
