Thank you for this, and for your patience. I want to be honest about where we are. The steering committee has not yet decided whether outside mentors fit the pilot this year. Our own mentors are still settling into a shared way of working, and there is some concern that adding a second kind of mentor before that settles could confuse students and families about who does what.

That said, your focus on career exploration and college planning is exactly the area where our mentors are weakest right now, so I do not want to close the door.

I am bringing this to the committee on October 23. I will come back to you the week after with a clear answer. If the answer is yes, I think the right shape is a separate time slot rather than inside our mentor block, so the two roles stay distinct, but that is my view and not a decision.

Can you hold the twelve volunteers until the end of October?
