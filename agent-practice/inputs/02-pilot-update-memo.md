# Pilot update memo: Compass mentoring, first six weeks

_Sample document for agent practice. The organization, people, and numbers are fictional._

**To:** Hillcrest leadership team
**From:** Priya
**Date:** October 2

## The headline

The structure is working. The content inside it is not yet consistent.

## What we are seeing

Attendance in the mentor block is 96 percent, higher than attendance in any other period of the day. Students show up. Mentors show up. The schedule has held for six weeks without an exception, which is the first time a new block has survived the fall at Hillcrest.

What happens inside the block varies a lot. Our check-in log shows that mentors who use a structured conversation protocol record specific goals for their students, and those students can name their goal when asked. Mentors who run the block as open time record very little, and when we asked a sample of their students what they were working toward, most could not say.

## What we changed

Starting this week, every mentor uses the same five-question goal-setting protocol. This was decided at the September 18 steering meeting. Dana added a "protocol used" field to the log so we can see uptake by the end of October.

## What is still unresolved

We still do not have an agreed measure for whether students are known well. The check-in log tells us what mentors did. It does not tell us what students experienced. I think we need a short student survey, but I have not proposed one yet because I do not want to add another thing to mentors' plates before the protocol settles.

The community partner question is also still open. Two organizations want to place volunteers in the spring. My instinct is to wait until we can describe our own mentor role clearly before adding outside mentors, but this is a leadership decision and I want to hear your thinking.

## What I need from you

A decision by the end of October on whether to pursue the student survey this semester or wait until spring. I will bring two options to the October 23 meeting.
